About PRISM

PRISM searches three public collections: GDELT for news, OpenAlex for scholarly publications, and Wikipedia for reference articles.

Results link directly to their original sources. The text inspector looks for useful details in text you paste.

Search coverage depends on those collections. Requests go directly from your browser to their services.
